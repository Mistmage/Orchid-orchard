# Final goal — Orchid Orchard as a local-first knowledge/workspace OS

The end-state from `Orchid project.md`: a recursive record/block store where
tabs, notes, bookmarks, history, snapshots, and annotations are all records
with typed content and typed edges. One store, one policy engine, one export
format, many views (prose, canvas, calendar, graph, browser).

Includes: graded root permissions, link/query duality, block-level incremental
writes, Wisteria record-native browser, Magic Paper canvas view.
